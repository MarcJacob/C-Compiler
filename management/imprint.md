Root folder for human and AI-oriented management notes, resources and plans.

`AI_Work_Guidance.md` describes how an AI model should advise the developer on their next development step (e.g. "what should I work on next") — read it when handling that kind of request.

`dev-plan.md` is the current overall development plan, tracking steps and their completion state ([DONE] / [WIP]) plus a backlog. It's the primary reference for "what's next" and should be kept up to date per the status-pass rules in `AI_Work_Guidance.md`.
